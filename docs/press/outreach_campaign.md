# FBA-Bench B2B Outreach Campaign

## Target Audience Segments

### Segment 1: AI Agent Framework Startups (The "Tool Builders")
**Profile:** Companies like CrewAI, LangChain, AutoGen, LlamaIndex, or newer YC startups building the underlying agentic orchestration layers.
**Their Pain Point:** They need to prove *their* framework is more reliable and robust than the competition for enterprise use cases.
**The Offer:** "Let's benchmark your default framework on FBA-Bench. A strong score gives you the ultimate enterprise case study."

### Segment 2: Applied AI Labs / Enterprise Innovation Teams
**Profile:** Teams within Fortune 500s or well-funded startups building autonomous SDRs, automated supply chain bots, or high-level strategic reasoning agents (e.g., Cognition, Devin competitors, specialized vertical agents).
**Their Pain Point:** They are terrified to deploy because of compounding hallucinations and security risks.
**The Offer:** "Before you deploy, let us run your agent through our Red Team Gauntlet and 365-day economic simulation to guarantee it won't bankrupt you."

**Subject:** The "Loss Porn" stress test & your agent's survival

I've been following [Company]'s work on autonomous agents, and your architecture looks incredibly promising.

We built FBA-Bench: a 365-day, highly adversarial economic simulation backed by a strict GAAP double-entry ledger. It’s the closest thing to an enterprise "crash test" for AI. Most models go bankrupt in 14 days because they hallucinate margins or fall for our Red Team Gauntlet (phishing and compliance traps).

**We also run a stress test called "Loss Porn"** (the AI actually named to this). We inject 75bps rate hikes and active supply chain delays to force your agent into a severe liquidity crisis, to test its actual cognitive resilience.

We are taking on a few private audits this month for teams looking to prove their agent's safety before production deployment. You’ll get a 20-page Integrity Report and full Godot visual replays of the agent's failure modes.

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute chat to see if a private run makes sense for [Company]?

Best,
